Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Charlotte Hayes ("Wellira", "we", "us") handles information when you visit wellira.online. We keep this short and specific, because a privacy policy nobody reads protects nobody.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell anything directly on this site. As a result we collect very little:

  • Basic technical data that your browser sends automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, the page you visited and the page that referred you. This is standard server log information.
  • Messages you choose to send us, including your email address and whatever you write in it.

We do not collect payment information. Purchases happen on the manufacturer's website, under their own privacy policy, and your card details never pass through this site.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what is necessary for the site to function and to understand roughly how many people visit and which pages they read.

Product videos are embedded from third-party platforms. Those players are not loaded until you click play: so if you never start a video, that provider sets nothing on your device through this site.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The site will continue to work.

Third-party websites and products

When you click a product link, you leave this site and continue on the manufacturer's or retailer's website. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content, their privacy practices, or the products they sell. Their policies apply from the moment you arrive there, and we encourage you to read them.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection and we take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
