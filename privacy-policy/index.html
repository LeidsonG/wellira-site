Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Charlotte Hayes ("Wellira", "we", "us") handles information when you visit wellira.example. We keep this short and specific, because a privacy policy nobody reads protects nobody.

What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell anything directly on this site. As a result we collect very little:

  • Basic technical data that your browser sends automatically: IP address, browser type, device type, the page you visited and the page that referred you. This is standard server log information.
  • Messages you choose to send us, including your email address and whatever you write in it.

We do not collect payment information. Purchases happen on the manufacturer's website, under their own privacy policy, and your card details never pass through this site.

How we use information

For ordinary, practical things: keeping the site online, answering messages you send us, and seeing which articles people actually find useful so we write more of those.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your email address with the brands we write about, and nobody pays us for access to your inbox.

Cookies and similar technologies

Some cookies keep the site working. Others help us count visits and understand which pages get read. You can block or delete all of them in your browser settings, and the site will still work.

Product videos are embedded from third-party platforms. Those players are not loaded until you click play: so if you never start a video, that provider sets nothing on your device through this site.

Analytics and advertising

We use measurement tools from companies such as Google and Meta to see how people find the site and which articles they read, and to show what we publish to people with similar interests. These tools set cookies and receive technical details of your visit, such as your IP address and the pages you opened.

What they report back to us is a count, not a name: that an article was read, not who read it. If you would rather not see personalized ads, you can turn them off in your Google and Meta account settings or at optout.aboutads.info.

Third-party websites and products

When you click a product link, you leave this site and continue on the manufacturer's or retailer's website. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their content, their privacy practices, or the products they sell. Their policies apply from the moment you arrive there, and we encourage you to read them.

How long we keep it

Server logs are kept for a short period and then discarded. Messages you send stay in the inbox as long as we need them to answer you properly. If you would rather we deleted yours sooner, just say so.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of whatever we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Wherever you live, we will do it, and asking never changes how we treat you.

One email to charlottehayesfb@gmail.com is enough, no form and no account. If you live in California or another U.S. state with its own privacy law, these are rights you hold by law and we answer within the time that law allows.

Children

We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has sent us something, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection and we take reasonable measures to protect the limited information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If any of this changes, we will update this page.
